Slop Anything LITE app icon (craft-anything.svg and every file derived from it
in this directory: craft-anything-1024.png, craft-anything.icns,
craft-anything.ico and hicolor/).

This is the sparkles icon, drawn for this fork. It is covered by this
repository's MIT licence (see LICENSE-MIT at the repository root), Copyright

It replaces the upstream Craft Anything icon (the raven, made by the ArtCraft
team), which was not open source and whose terms required forks and modified
versions to replace it with their own icon. The raven files are removed from
this fork; the removal is recorded in docs/slop/PROVENANCE.md.
